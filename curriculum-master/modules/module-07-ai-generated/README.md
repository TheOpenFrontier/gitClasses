# Module 07: Advanced Recursion

Welcome to Module 07 of gitClasses! In this module, we will explore the fascinating world of advanced recursion. By the end of this module, you will have a solid understanding of recursive algorithms and how to implement them effectively.

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Understand the concept of recursion and its applications.
- Implement advanced recursive algorithms such as Fibonacci sequence and factorial computation.
- Utilize recursion to solve complex problems in a clearer, more structured way.
- Apply best practices for writing recursive functions in Python.

## Paths
This module offers three paths tailored to meet your learning style:

**Path A: Guided Learning**
- Follow step-by-step instructions in the starter code.
- Engage with provided resources and complete exercises along the way.

**Path B: Problem Solving**
- Solve a series of advanced recursive problems on your own.
- Submit your solutions for feedback and code
